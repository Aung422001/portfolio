What I'd pick

  • Right now, with placeholders: D — the index list. It is the only style that looks deliberately minimal rather than unfinished when there are no screenshots, it fits all five projects on one screen, and it needs no JavaScript. Pair it with your existing featured hero for Tri-Apex and you get hierarchy plus compactness.
  • Once you add screenshots: C — bento. It is the most current-looking of the six and shows off images without the page getting long. Not before the images exist, though.
  • B — scroll-stacking is the most fun and suits the reference's style, but it gives all five projects equal weight, which undersells Tri-Apex as your only professional work.
  • E — alternating rows is what I'd use if you cut to three projects and wrote each up as a proper case study. Strongest for depth, weakest for scanning.
  • Skip F. Horizontal rails hide work behind a gesture, and recruiters do not hunt.
